Carrier onboarding checks

Before a freight broker keys a new carrier into its TMS, this script checks the carrier against FMCSA's public records and the broker's carrier rules, then sorts it into ready for TMS entry, the exception queue, or do not set up. Every check logs the fields it read.

Why it matters

FMCSA moved operating-authority records to its new registration system (MOTUS) in 2026. In the live file, many long-standing carriers now show a 2026 grant date, so a check that reads the latest grant calls them a few months old and refuses them under a six-month rule. The full checks treat those migration rows as noise, restart the clock only on a real break, and send a carrier to a person when the records cannot settle its grant date.

The 2026 grant dates

Every carrier below has a grant or reinstatement dated 2026 in the live AuthHist file, so all four fall inside the six-month window. Only two of them are actually new.

Other choices

  • Cargo is checked on the certificate only, because FMCSA does not hold cargo filings for general freight.
  • Liability below the broker's $1,000,000 rule but at the federal minimum goes to a person when the certificate claims the higher limit, so the agent can confirm the excess policy.
  • A contact mismatch never rejects. It routes the carrier to a person who calls back on the FMCSA phone number. Free-mail domains prove nothing, so for those only the phone counts.
  • A suspension notice in the last 12 months goes to a person even if the authority is active again.

Data

FMCSA records for 8 real carriers from data.transportation.gov (Company Census, Motus Carrier, AuthHist, RevokeSuspend, Out of Service Orders) and the legacy AuthHist archive, captured 2026-11-18, emails masked. The packets (phone, email, certificate amounts) are constructed; cases 4 and 5 are deliberately altered and say nothing about those carriers. The rules are one broker's public carrier requirements. A decision reflects only FMCSA's files on that date against those rules, not a judgment of any carrier.

Run it yourself

Python 3.9+, standard library only.

python3 run_checks.py
python3 run_checks.py --naive
python3 run_checks.py --source live
